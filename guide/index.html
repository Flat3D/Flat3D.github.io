Quick answers

What is the GTA 6 map?

The GTA 6 map is Leonida, a Florida-inspired state in Grand Theft Auto VI. Published regions include Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga. This site is an unofficial interactive atlas of those regions.

Where can I find an interactive GTA 6 map?

This GTA 6 map lets you pan Leonida, filter by region, and open places and collectables from the Leonida map: shops, robberies, stunts, races, and collectible cards. It is a fan atlas, not an official Rockstar product.

Is Vice City on the GTA 6 map?

Yes. Vice City is the urban core of the GTA 6 map, on Leonida’s coast. Use the Vice City region filter on this atlas to show only city pins such as the street grid, beaches, and port.

What regions are on the GTA 6 map of Leonida?

Rockstar has named Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga. This GTA 6 map groups every pin under those six regions so you can isolate one area at a time.

Is this the official Rockstar GTA 6 map?

No. GTA 6 Map is an unofficial fan atlas of Leonida. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

How do I share a location on this GTA 6 map?

Open any pin, then use Share or copy the address bar. The link restores the selected place, layers, region, and search so another player lands on the same Leonida view.
