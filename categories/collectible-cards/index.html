Common questions

How many Collectible Cards are on this GTA 6 map?

This atlas currently lists 90 Collectible Cards. That count is for this unofficial fan map, not an official total from Grand Theft Auto VI.

How do I show only Collectible Cards on the GTA 6 map?

Open the interactive map and turn on the Collectible Cards layer, or use the link on this page that enables that layer. Other categories stay hidden until you switch them back on.

How do I mark Collectible Cards as found?

Open a pin and save a field note. Found pins stay in this browser after a refresh. You can download the list as JSON and load it back later.
